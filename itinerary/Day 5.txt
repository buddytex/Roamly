Day 5: Hotel Checkout & Return Journey

1. Wake up and pack
7:00–7:30 AM
Location: Hotel 1900 Chinatown (proposed)
Get ready, pack all belongings, check the room and keep your passport, phone and flight documents accessible.

2. Breakfast
7:30–8:00 AM
Location: Hotel or nearby Chinatown café
Have breakfast and check the airline's baggage allowance and check-in requirements.

3. Hotel checkout
8:00–8:30 AM
Location: Hotel 1900 Chinatown (proposed)
Return the room key, settle any outstanding charges and confirm that all luggage has been collected. Ask about luggage storage if your flight is later in the day.

4. Transfer to Changi Airport
8:30–9:30 AM — illustrative
Destination: Singapore Changi Airport (SIN)
Take the agency-arranged vehicle or MRT. Allow extra time for traffic, luggage and the terminal associated with the actual flight.

5. Airline check-in and baggage drop
At the airline's recommended arrival time
Location: Your airline's departure terminal at Changi Airport
Check in if required, drop checked baggage, verify the boarding gate and complete departure procedures. For many international flights, arriving around three hours before departure is a useful guideline; follow the airline's instructions.

6. Security, immigration and boarding gate
Before the scheduled departure
Location: Changi Airport departure area
Complete the required formalities, check the airport displays for gate changes and reach the boarding gate by the airline's stated deadline.

7. Return flight
As per confirmed flight ticket
From: Singapore Changi Airport (SIN) · To: Agency-booked destination airport
Board the confirmed flight home. Roamly should display the actual airline, flight number, departure time and arrival time from the agency's booking record.
