Day 3 timeline

1. Wake up and breakfast
7:00–8:00 AM
Location: Hotel 1900 Chinatown (proposed)
Have breakfast, apply sunscreen, carry water and wear comfortable walking shoes.

2. Travel to Sentosa
8:00–9:30 AM
Destination: Resorts World Sentosa, 8 Sentosa Gateway, Singapore
Travel by MRT to HarbourFront, then follow signs to VivoCity and the Sentosa Express, or take a taxi. Allow extra time for walking and entry.

3. Arrive at Universal Studios Singapore
9:30–10:00 AM
Location: Universal Studios Singapore, 8 Sentosa Gateway
Reach the entrance, check the day's opening time, prepare your ticket and review the park map. The actual gate-opening time may vary.

4. Explore Universal Studios
10:00 AM–1:00 PM
Location: Universal Studios Singapore
Explore the themed zones and choose rides according to height requirements, queues and your interests. Prioritize your must-see attractions early.

5. Lunch break
1:00–2:00 PM
Location: Dining area inside Universal Studios Singapore
Have lunch, hydrate and rest. Check whether outside food is permitted before bringing any.

6. Continue the park visit
2:00–5:00 PM
Location: Universal Studios Singapore
Visit remaining attractions, explore themed areas and take photos. Keep the last 30 minutes flexible for queues and exit.

7. Relax at Sentosa Beach
5:30–6:30 PM
Location: Siloso Beach, Sentosa Island
Walk along the beach, enjoy the sea views and take a break after the theme park. Adjust for sunset, weather and travel time.

8. Optional Singapore Cable Car
6:30–7:30 PM
Location: Siloso Point or another suitable Sentosa cable car station
Enjoy views across the island and harbour if the service is operating and tickets are available. Verify the final operating time and allow for station transfers.

9. Dinner and return
7:30–9:00 PM
Location: VivoCity or near the hotel
Have dinner, return to Chinatown by MRT or taxi, and rest for Day 4.
