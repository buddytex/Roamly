Day 1 schedule

1. Arrive at Changi Airport
2:00–3:30 PM · Illustrative

Location: Singapore Changi Airport (SIN)
- Immigration and baggage collection.
- Connect to airport Wi-Fi or activate your travel SIM.
- Meet the agency-arranged driver or follow the planned public transport route.
Allow roughly 60–90 minutes for airport formalities as a planning estimate; actual time varies.

2. Transfer to the hotel (https://maps.app.goo.gl/odBD2yfuwh9PkTHy6)
3:30–4:30 PM

Location: Hotel 1900 Chinatown, Singapore
- Travel to the hotel; allow approximately 30–60 minutes depending on transport and traffic.
- Check in, leave luggage and freshen up.
- Confirm the actual property address in the agency booking.

3. Chinatown food stop
5:00–6:00 PM

Location: Maxwell Food Centre, 1 Kadayanallur Street, Singapore 069184
- Sample local hawker food.
- Keep this meal flexible if the flight is delayed.
- Check individual stall hours before visiting.

4. Merlion Park
6:15–7:00 PM

Location: Merlion Park, Fullerton Road, Singapore
- Photograph the Merlion statue.
- Enjoy views across the bay.
- Walk along the waterfront toward Marina Bay Sands if time and weather permit.

5. Marina Bay Sands waterfront
7:00–8:00 PM

Location: Event Plaza, 10 Bayfront Avenue, Singapore
- Walk around the waterfront and take skyline photos.
- Find a viewing spot for Spectra, the free light-and-water show.
- The official schedule lists 15-minute performances; verify the showtime for the actual visit date.

6. Night skyline and return
8:15–9:30 PM

Location: Marina Bay waterfront, then hotel
- Enjoy the illuminated skyline and take final photos.
- Have a light snack if needed.
- Return to the hotel and rest for Day 2.
