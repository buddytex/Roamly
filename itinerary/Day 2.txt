Day 2: Gardens by the Bay

1. Wake up and breakfast
7:00–8:00 AM
Location: Hotel 1900 Chinatown (proposed)
Get ready, have breakfast and pack water, a phone charger and comfortable walking shoes.

2. Travel to Gardens by the Bay
8:00–9:00 AM
Destination: 18 Marina Gardens Drive, Singapore 018953
Travel by MRT via Bayfront station or use a taxi. Allow time to walk to the conservatory entrance.

3. Explore Cloud Forest
9:00–10:30 AM
Location: Cloud Forest conservatory
See the indoor waterfall, tropical plants and elevated walkways. The attraction's published hours are 9:00 AM–9:00 PM, with last admission at 8:00 PM.

4. Visit Flower Dome
10:30 AM–12:00 PM
Location: Flower Dome conservatory
Explore the seasonal floral displays and themed gardens. The official recommended visit duration is around one hour.

5. Lunch and rest
12:00–1:00 PM
Location: Jurassic Nest Food Hall or another on-site dining option
Have lunch, refill water and take a short break before outdoor sightseeing.

6. Explore Supertree Grove
1:00–2:30 PM
Location: Supertree Grove, Gardens by the Bay
Walk among the iconic Supertrees and take photos. The outdoor gardens are a good flexible option if you want to slow down.

7. Optional OCBC Skyway
2:30–3:30 PM
Location: OCBC Skyway
Walk above the gardens for elevated views if tickets are available. This is optional and weather-dependent.

8. Free time and refreshments
3:30–6:30 PM
Location: Gardens by the Bay
Rest, explore outdoor areas, visit the gift shop or have an early dinner. Keep this period flexible for heat, rain and walking breaks.

9. Garden Rhapsody light show
7:45–8:00 PM
Location: Supertree Grove
Watch the free outdoor music-and-light show. The official published showtimes are 7:45 PM and 8:45 PM daily; check the schedule close to your visit.

10. Return to the hotel
8:15–9:00 PM
Destination: Hotel 1900 Chinatown (proposed)
Travel back by MRT or taxi, then rest before the Sentosa trip on Day 3.
