Day 4: Culture & City Exploration

1. Wake up and breakfast
7:30–8:30 AM
Location: Hotel 1900 Chinatown (proposed)
Get ready, have breakfast and prepare for a walking-focused day. Carry water and dress comfortably.

2. Explore Chinatown
8:30–9:30 AM
Location: Pagoda Street and Chinatown, Singapore
Walk along the traditional shophouses, browse local shops and photograph the colourful streets. Keep shopping brief so you have enough time for the temple.

3. Visit Buddha Tooth Relic Temple
9:30–10:30 AM
Location: 288 South Bridge Road, Singapore 058840
Explore the temple's architecture and cultural displays where visitors are permitted. Check current visitor hours and dress respectfully.

4. Lunch in Chinatown
10:45 AM–12:00 PM
Location: Maxwell Food Centre, 1 Kadayanallur Street, Singapore 069184
Enjoy local hawker food before travelling to Kampong Glam. Check individual stall hours and choose food according to your dietary preferences.

5. Visit Kampong Glam and Sultan Mosque
12:00–1:30 PM
Location: Sultan Mosque, 3 Muscat Street, Singapore 198833
Explore the historic district and admire the mosque from the designated visitor areas. Check mosque visiting hours and prayer-time restrictions before planning entry.

6. Explore Haji Lane
1:30–3:00 PM
Location: Haji Lane, Kampong Glam, Singapore
Discover street murals, independent boutiques, cafés and photo spots. Shop or stop for a drink if the weather is hot.

7. Free time and café break
3:00–5:00 PM
Location: Arab Street and surrounding Kampong Glam streets
Browse textile shops, take photos and rest at a café. This buffer allows for slower walking and spontaneous stops.

8. Dinner
5:30–7:00 PM
Location: Kampong Glam or Bugis, Singapore
Enjoy dinner at a nearby restaurant. Confirm opening hours and dietary options before choosing a specific venue.

9. Return to the hotel
7:00–8:00 PM
Destination: Hotel 1900 Chinatown (proposed)
Return by MRT or taxi, relax and prepare your luggage for the final day.
